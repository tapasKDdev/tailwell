Affiliate disclosure

In plain language: some of the links on this site are affiliate links. This page explains exactly what that means, what we earn, and — most importantly — what it never changes.

How affiliate links work

When you click a link on TailWell and buy a product from one of our retail partners, that partner may pay us a small commission. The price you pay is identical whether or not you use our link — shopping with us never costs you a cent more. On a typical purchase, our commission is a small percentage of the sale price.

Where you'll see it

Any article that contains affiliate links carries a short disclosure note near the top, and the relevant product recommendation boxes are clearly marked with a badge such as "Our pick." We treat our disclosure like a promise we'd rather over-deliver on: if a recommendation could reasonably be influenced by money, you'll be able to see that from us plainly linking and labeling it.

What our commission never changes

  • Rankings. Products are selected and ordered based on our research criteria — dose transparency, testing, evidence, and value — long before any affiliate relationship is considered. We never charge companies to appear in a guide.
  • What we say. If a product we earn from has a weakness, we say so. We don't run "best of" lists sponsored by a brand, and we don't write reviews on commission.
  • Our research process. The steps in our editorial process happen exactly the same way with or without links attached.

How TailWell is funded

Affiliate commissions are our primary source of income, which is why we're able to publish everything free to read. The trade-off is one we take seriously: our trust is the only reason the links exist, so we guard it the way the links' income depends on it — because it does.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or things that feel off? We'd rather hear it than never know. Reach us any time on the contact page.
