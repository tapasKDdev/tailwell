About us

We research so you don't have to.

TailWell is a small, independent team of pet owners, writers, and researchers. We publish buying guides and honest reviews for pet families — with a particular focus on the senior and health-sensitive pets whose owners need genuinely reliable answers.

Why we started TailWell

Most pet-product content on the internet is written to sell a product, not to answer a question. Rankings are bought, doses are hidden, and "studies show" is attached to things no study ever said. We started TailWell because we wanted the guide we wished existed when our own dogs got older: calm, generous, evidence-aware advice we could act on without feeling sold to.

What we publish

Buying guides and comparisons, first and foremost — shopping decisions are where owners feel most at sea. We also publish longer, wellness-focused articles on senior pet health, nutrition, and how to make daily care easier. Everything is written for the moment someone is actually deciding what to buy, feed, or try next.

How we research

Every guide on this site follows the same editorial process:

  1. We read the primary evidence first. Published veterinary trials, systematic reviews, and ingredient fact sheets — before any marketing material.
  2. We talk to the right people. Veterinary nutritionists, behaviorists, and clinicians who work with senior pets every day.
  3. We judge on label facts. Printed doses, third-party testing, cost per effective dose, and honest comparisons of what's inside.
  4. We disclose everything. When a link earns us a commission, you'll know. Rankings are chosen before and independent of any affiliate consideration.
  5. We update on a schedule. Guides carry dates and are re-reviewed at least twice a year as evidence and products change.

No sponsored rankings. No paying to appear. No "best of" lists put together from a press release and a product photo.

Who's behind it

We're a small group — but on the "small" side on purpose. Every guide is edited by a human who understands both the research and what it's like at 6 a.m. with a stiff old dog who needs help into the car. We're pet owners first, writers second. The affiliate partner is a distant third and the algorithm a distant fourth.

The pets we think about

A friendly reminder between us: TailWell content is educational and should never replace a veterinarian's advice. When a decision matters for a health-sensitive pet, we'll say so — and we'll always recommend talking to your own vet before or while you shop.
