Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: we collect as little as possible, we don't sell your data, and we only use the standard analytics and advertising tools that keep the site free.

What we collect

TailWell does not require an account, and reading the site doesn't require you to share anything with us directly. In the background, like most websites, we use standard tools — an analytics service for anonymized traffic stats and, in the future, advertising or affiliate tracking cookies. These tools may record things like your browser type, referring page, and pages you visit. This data is aggregated and used to understand what readers find useful.

Affiliate links

Our affiliate partners may set cookies when you click their links so they can credit a referral. These cookies are controlled by the partner and governed by their privacy policies — not ours. We don't see, store, or receive your purchase data; we only receive a referral credit when a purchase is completed.

Cookies

We use only the minimal cookies required for these tools. We deliberately avoid: aggressive ad networks, pop-up or exit-intent scripts that track you, and any third-party scripts that don't need to be there. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time, and the site will keep working.

Email contact

If you email us through the contact page, we'll only use your address to reply. We don't add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent, and we don't share addresses with anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold. Since we hold almost none directly, requests are usually quick to resolve. For any privacy question, email hello@tailwell.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and change the date below. Material changes are always announced clearly.
