## Why TailWell exists

Pet wellness is a crowded category. Every brand claims the top spot, every influencer has a "must-have," and every Google search returns a wall of competing — often contradictory — advice. TailWell exists to cut through that noise.

We are an independent pet care wellness resource. Across five topic areas — wellness & health, food & nutrition, gear & tech, training & behavior, and senior & special-needs care — we research the products and practices that genuinely help pets, compare them against clear criteria, and tell you where our information comes from.

## What we do

- **We research first, recommend second.** Each product recommendation is checked against our published [Review Methodology](/review-methodology/) — a written standard that covers safety, evidence, price, and real-world usability.
- **We say no to garbage.** Products we can't verify, brands without transparent ingredient lists, or "miracle" claims with no science behind them do not appear in our articles.
- **We disclose everything.** Some links on this site are affiliate links — we may earn a small commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. That never changes what we recommend. See our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for the full policy.

## Editorial standards

1. **Accuracy:** We verify claims against primary sources — peer-reviewed studies, manufacturer documentation, and veterinary guidance — and we date-stamp our reviews.
2. **Independence:** We are not paid for placement. Our revenue comes from affiliate commissions and (in the future) independent sponsorship, never from vendors buying a review.
3. **Transparency:** If a recommendation is based on limited evidence, we say so. If we are unsure, we tell you what to ask your veterinarian.
4. **Correction:** When we get something wrong, we fix it quickly and note the correction in the article — see our [Corrections Policy](/corrections-policy/).

The full version of these standards lives on our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/) page.

## A note on veterinary care

TailWell is an information resource, not a veterinary practice. Nothing on this site is a substitute for professional veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your veterinarian before starting any new supplement, food, or treatment plan — especially for senior, pregnant, or special-needs pets.
