## Advertising Policy

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

### The core promise

**TailWell does not sell placement.** Nobody can pay for a rank, a spot in a list, a mention, or a mention's position. Editorial judgment is not for sale, and it never will be — that is the whole reason the site is worth reading.

### Where our money comes from

- **Affiliate commissions.** When you buy through our links, retailers may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. Full details: [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
- **Advertising: none today.** The Site displays no third-party advertising, no ad tags, and no sponsored posts.

### Rules that govern endorsements and rankings (US and UK)

We hold ourselves to the advertising rules in the markets we serve:

- **US — FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255):** any material connection between us and a brand must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously; endorsements must reflect honest opinions and cannot say anything the evidence does not support.
- **US — FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials (16 CFR Part 465, in force since October 21, 2024):** we do not publish reviews, ratings, or testimonials that we know to be fake, AI-generated, or bought — and we do not buy them.
- **UK — CAP Code rules 2.1 and 2.3 (ASA):** commercial content must be obviously identifiable as such, upfront — not discovered through a footer disclaimer after the fact. That is why our affiliate disclosure sits **above** the links on every article page, not just in the footer, and why vague wording like "this link *might* earn us something" is banned here.

### If we ever run ads or sponsored content

Nothing below is active. It is the rulebook we would have to follow first:

1. **Label before engagement.** A clear, prominent label ("Advertisement" / "Sponsored") appears at the top of the sponsored unit — visible before a reader clicks or scrolls, never only at the page foot.
2. **Sponsored ≠ ranked.** A sponsor never appears in a ranked list, a "best" title, or a comparison table as a result of the sponsorship. Money and rankings stay separate, always.
3. **Same standards.** Sponsored pieces obey the same evidence and claim rules as everything else — the [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/) does not relax for paying partners.
4. **Cookies first, ads second.** Under UK PECR, no non-essential advertising cookie loads before you have consented. If ads are added, the [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) are updated in the same session.
5. **Everything disclosed** here and in the [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) before launch.

### Fake content — never

We do not create, buy, or publish:

- fake or incentivized-positive reviews,
- invented five-star ratings or review counts,
- fabricated testimonials or quotes,
- AI-generated "customer" feedback,
- or company-written endorsements presented as independent.

Reviews and ratings on this Site, if any, will be tied to verifiable sources — nothing is staged.

### Brand and PR requests

A brand asking for favorable coverage gets the same answer every time: coverage follows the [Review Methodology](/review-methodology/), and the criteria are published. Brands may send factual corrections (with sources); they cannot buy or edit copy. Report anything else via the [Contact](/contact/) page.
