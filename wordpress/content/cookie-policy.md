## Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

### The short version

**TailWell does not set any cookies.** There is no analytics cookie, no advertising cookie, no social pixel, and no preference cookie on this Site. You can use every page without consenting to anything.

### What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store. Similar technologies (local storage, tracking pixels, device fingerprinting) work in the same space. In the UK, the rules covering them are the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR); in the US, cookie practices fall under general consumer-protection law. This policy explains how they apply here.

### Cookies set by TailWell: none

We have deliberately built the Site that way:

- **No analytics cookies.** We do not use Google Analytics or any traffic tool that tags your browser. (See §2 of the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for the anonymous server logs we do keep.)
- **No advertising cookies.** We do not run ads or ad tags on the Site.
- **No tracking pixels.** No social media or advertising network can see that you visited us.

Because nothing non-essential is stored on your device, there is no cookie banner to dismiss and no consent to give. We think that is how it should be — and it means we cannot use "we got consent" as an excuse for anything.

### Cookies you may encounter anyway

- **On retailer sites.** Clicking a product link takes you to a retailer (Amazon, Chewy, and similar). Those sites may set cookies to track the referral and their own purposes. Their policies govern them, not ours. Details: [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
- **Comment cookies (only if commenting is enabled on an article).** WordPress remembers your name, email, and website (if you fill them in) so you do not have to retype them next time. These last up to one year (comment token: two weeks). You can clear them whenever you like without losing anything.
- **Your own browser.** Your browser may store things on your own device for its own features (form autofill, offline data). That is between you and your browser.

### If we ever add optional cookies

If TailWell introduces analytics, advertising, or personalization, we will:

1. update this policy and the privacy policy first,
2. show a consent mechanism that lets you refuse non-essential cookies before they load, and
3. make sure withdrawal of consent is as easy as giving it.

We will never load non-essential cookies before you have agreed to them.

### Managing cookies

Almost every browser lets you block, delete, or inspect cookies in its settings. Blocking cookies will not break TailWell — the Site works without any.
