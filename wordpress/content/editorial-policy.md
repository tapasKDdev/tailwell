## Editorial Policy

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

This policy says how TailWell content is chosen, checked, and updated — and where the line is that we will not cross.

### Our independence rules

1. **No payment for placement.** No company can buy a position, a rank, a mention, or a review on TailWell. We do not sell links, and we do not accept payment, free products, or other incentives in exchange for coverage.
2. **Affiliate commissions do not decide picks.** We earn commissions through affiliate links (see [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/)), but commission availability is not a selection criterion. If a product with no affiliate program deserves the pick, it gets the pick.
3. **No sponsored rankings.** Until we introduce clearly labeled sponsorship (see [Advertising Policy](/advertising-policy/)), there is no sponsored content on this Site at all. If that ever changes, the label will be obvious and upfront — never a footnote.
4. **Brands do not review our copy.** We do not let manufacturers preview, approve, or edit what we write about them.

### How content gets made

- Every article follows the standards in our [Review Methodology](/review-methodology/): published criteria, evidence-first checking, and a plain-language summary of why each pick made the list.
- **Sources are tiered.** We prioritize peer-reviewed studies and regulatory guidance, then veterinary-university and national veterinary organization material. Manufacturer material is used for product specifications (ingredients, sizes, price) — never as proof that a product works. Blogs, forums, and Wikipedia are not evidence (Wikipedia may be used as background to find primary sources, never cited).
- **Nothing is invented.** Statistics, studies, quotes, credentials, and product specs must trace to a real source. If we cannot verify a claim, the claim does not appear.
- **Health content carries the vet line.** Health-adjacent articles remind readers to consult their veterinarian, and nothing is framed as a diagnosis, cure, or treatment directive.
- **No fake voices.** We do not invent authors, experts, veterinarians, testimonial writers, reviewers, or ratings. Content is produced by the TailWell editorial team, with AI used only as a drafting assistant under the rules in our internal AI content policy — AI output never counts as a source.

### Conflicts of interest

- The Site's revenue is affiliate commissions (and, potentially in the future, clearly labeled advertising). That is disclosed on the [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
- We do not accept affiliate deals that require favorable coverage, exclusivity, or approval rights.
- If we ever cover a competitor's product or a brand we have a relationship with, we say so in the article.

### What we do not publish

- Claims that a product treats, cures, prevents, or reverses a disease or condition (including implied versions like "supports immunity" when we cannot back it).
- "Clinically proven," "veterinarian approved," "vet-recommended," "lab-tested," "risk-free," or similar words — unless the specific, verifiable fact behind them is cited in that sentence.
- Fake scarcity, fake urgency, fake reviews, fake five-star ratings, or fabricated testimonials.
- Copied manufacturer or retailer marketing copy presented as our own.

### Corrections and updates

Errors happen; hiding them is what we will not do. See our [Corrections Policy](/corrections-policy/) for how to report a problem and how we fix it.

### Contact

Disagree with something we published? Use the [Contact](/contact/) page — substantive challenges are welcome and get read.
