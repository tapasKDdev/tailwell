## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

TailWell ("we", "us", "our") operates this website (the "Site"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

**The short version:** we do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking software on this Site. We collect only what you send us (through the contact form) plus routine server logs needed to keep the Site online. We never sell personal information. Details are below.

### 1. Information you give us

- **Contact form.** If you message us through the [Contact](/contact/) page, we receive your name, email address, and message content. That is only used to answer you.
- **Newsletter — not active yet.** The newsletter box on the homepage is presentational: no email address is sent or stored anywhere. If we ever launch a real newsletter, we will update this policy and ask for consent where the law requires it before any address is collected.

### 2. Information collected automatically (server logs)

Like nearly every website, our hosting provider records basic request logs: your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser's user agent. We use these only for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting — not to build profiles of visitors. Logs stay with our host for its normal operational period and are not used by us for marketing.

### 3. Cookies and tracking technologies

**The Site itself sets no cookies.** There is no analytics script, no advertising tag, no social media pixel, and no tracking cookie on any TailWell page.

Things that are worth knowing anyway:

- **Affiliate links.** When you click a link to a retailer (for example Amazon or Chewy), the retailer may set a cookie on **its own site** to credit the referral. We do not control that cookie and we receive no personal information from it — only, at most, aggregate commission data. Details are on our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) page.
- **Comments (only if enabled on an article).** If commenting is turned on for a post, WordPress stores the comment itself, plus — if you choose — your name and email address in a browser cookie so it is remembered next time (the email cookie lasts one year, the name cookie one year, the comment cookie two weeks). You can clear these in your browser at any time.
- **Your browser controls.** You can block or delete cookies globally in your browser settings without affecting how TailWell works, because the Site does not depend on them.

Our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/) explains this in more detail, including the UK rules that apply.

### 4. Third-party services that load on the Site

Only one category of third-party request happens when you view a page:

- **Web fonts.** Typography is served from Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`). When your browser fetches the font, Google receives your IP address and basic request data as a matter of how that service works. Google does not receive anything else about you from us, and we send nothing about you to Google. If you prefer, you can block font requests in your browser — the Site will fall back to system fonts. *(We may switch to self-hosted fonts later to remove this request entirely; this policy will be updated if we do.)*

No visitor personal data from this Site is ever sent to AI or language-model providers. Our internal content-production tools (see `docs/privacy-data-inventory.md` for the internal data flows) work from editorial topics and drafts, not from anything you do on the Site.

### 5. How we use information

- To respond to messages you send us.
- To keep the Site secure and working (server logs).
- To meet legal obligations when one applies.

### 6. Affiliate relationships

We earn commissions through affiliate programs such as Amazon Associates and partnerships with select pet wellness brands. That is how the Site is funded. It never gives those partners your personal information, and we do not receive yours from them. Read the [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for the full picture.

### 7. Sharing and sale

We do **not** sell or share personal information for money or cross-context behavioral advertising — we have no advertising tags and no data brokers.

Limited disclosure happens only when needed to operate the Site:

| Who | What they get | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Our hosting provider | Server logs (including IPs) | To run and secure the Site |
| Google (fonts) | Your IP address, as part of a normal font request | To render typography (see §4) |
| Our email provider | Your message, delivered to our inbox | To let us answer you |

### 8. Retention

- **Contact messages:** kept for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, then deleted — sooner if you ask.
- **Server logs:** kept for our host's normal operational period, then rotated.
- We keep no other personal data about visitors.

### 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over any personal data we hold about you (which, for most visitors, means whatever you typed into the contact form):

- **UK / EEA residents:** rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 (as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025). You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
- **California residents:** the CCPA gives rights to know, delete, and correct personal information and to opt out of sale/sharing. We do not believe TailWell meets the CCPA's business thresholds (revenue, volume, or sale of data), and we do not sell or share personal information anyway — but we will honor qualifying requests voluntarily if you send one.
- **Other US states and countries:** if a privacy law applies to you, we will honor it the same way. Several US state laws take a similar "confirm/ddelete/correct" shape.

**How to exercise any right:** use the [Contact](/contact/) page. We will verify the request and respond within the time the law requires (about one month for UK requests). We never charge for a privacy request or retaliate against you for making one.

### 10. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

### 11. Third-party links

Our articles link to retailers, manufacturers, and other resources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control. Review them before sharing information.

### 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes will also be announced on the homepage.

### 13. Contact

Questions about this policy or a data request? Use the [Contact](/contact/) page.
