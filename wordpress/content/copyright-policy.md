## Copyright Policy

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

### 1. Our content

Articles, layout, and branding on TailWell are copyrighted by TailWell. You may:

- **quote short excerpts** with clear attribution and a link to the original article (UK: fair dealing for criticism, review, and quotation under the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 §29–30; US: fair use under 17 U.S.C. §107),
- **share links** freely, and
- **print articles** for personal, non-commercial use.

Anything beyond that — republishing whole articles, scraping content into your own site, training a commercial model on our corpus, or translating and reposting without permission — needs written permission first. Use the [Contact](/contact/) page.

### 2. Material that belongs to others

Our articles reference product names, trademarks, and short factual descriptions that belong to their owners. We use them for identification and commentary under the quotation/fair-dealing exceptions above, with attribution. Images and long passages from third parties are only used with permission or a license.

### 3. If we got something wrong

If you believe material on TailWell infringes your copyright — for example, text or an image we should not have used — contact us via the [Contact](/contact/) page with:

1. identification of the copyrighted work you say is infringed,
2. the exact URL (or enough detail to locate) the material you say is infringing,
3. your name and a way to reach you,
4. a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized, and
5. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and you are the rights holder (or authorized to act for them).

We aim to acknowledge reports within 7 days and to resolve meritorious claims promptly — normally by removing or properly licensing the material. We do not require you to use a particular form or lawyer's letter.

### 4. US DMCA designated agent

US safe-harbor law (DMCA §512) lets a service provider designate an agent with the U.S. Copyright Office to receive infringement notices. **TailWell has not yet registered a designated agent.** Until that registration exists, send notices through the [Contact](/contact/) page and we will handle them under the process in section 3. If commenting or other user-generated content is switched on at scale, registration will be completed and this section updated with the agent's details (registration must then also be published here).

### 5. Repeat infringement

We do not host third-party uploads as a core service, but if a party repeatedly submits infringing notices or posts infringing comments, we will block them from further submission.

### 6. Counter-notices and restoration

If you believe your material was removed in error, tell us via [Contact](/contact/) with the details of the material and why the removal was wrong. If the claim is resolved in your favor and the law requires restoration, we will restore it.
