## How We Review and Pick Products

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

Every "best," "top pick," and "our choice" on TailWell means one thing only: **the best match against the criteria below, judged by our editors at the time of the update.** It is not a scientific measurement of every product on earth, and nobody can buy the title.

### The five criteria

Each product in a guide is assessed against the same five headings:

1. **Safety and ingredient quality.** Is the label transparent? Are ingredients present in meaningful amounts? For supplements, are actives disclosed rather than hidden in a proprietary blend? Anything with a documented safety concern, or an ingredient list we cannot interpret, is out.
2. **Evidence quality.** What actually supports the claim — peer-reviewed research, regulatory guidance, veterinary-university material? Manufacturer material tells us what is *in* a product, not that it *works*. We say so when the evidence is thin.
3. **Price and value.** Price is compared against what you get (count, concentration, serving size), not sticker price alone. Prices change; each article shows when we last checked.
4. **Real-world usability.** Size options, dosing practicality, subscription lock-in, refill availability, build quality — the things owners actually live with.
5. **Label and company transparency.** Brands that hide sourcing, refuse to publish full ingredient panels, or lean on vague claims start behind.

### How a pick is made

- Products are screened against the criteria first; **affiliate availability is checked only after a product qualifies**, and never changes the outcome.
- Each pick gets a plain-language **"Why we picked it"** line and, where relevant, a **"Keep in mind"** limitation — no product is perfect, and we say what is not.
- When evidence conflicts, we say that rather than picking the louder side.
- **Only products we could verify appear.** Unverifiable products are dropped from the list entirely, not softened into vague praise.

### What the "Safety-reviewed" badge means

Some product cards carry a small **"Safety-reviewed"** badge. It means the row passed TailWell's internal safety gate: the product's category is health-adjacent, the label and claims were checked against the criteria above, and the row is approved for use in health-related copy.

**It does not mean a veterinarian examined, tested, or endorses the product.** We do not display veterinary, laboratory, or certification badges unless the underlying fact is real and documented for that specific product. For personal medical guidance about your own pet, your veterinarian is the right place — not a badge.

### What disqualifies a product

- Safety or regulatory flags we cannot resolve.
- Claims we cannot verify (including "clinically proven" without the study).
- Missing or non-transparent labeling.
- A brand refusing to answer basic sourcing or testing questions.
- Anything we would not consider for our own pets.

### How often this is re-checked

Articles are refreshed on a scheduled cadence, and prices are re-checked before each update. Dated "Updated" notes on an article tell you when the last pass happened — see our [Corrections Policy](/corrections-policy/) for how fixes are announced.
