## Affiliate Disclosure

**Effective date:** September 25, 2026

TailWell is reader-supported. When you buy through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission from the retailers we partner with — at no additional cost to you.

**As an Amazon Associate, TailWell earns from qualifying purchases.** We also participate in affiliate programs with select other pet wellness brands and retailers.

### Where you will see this disclosure

We do not rely on you finding this page by yourself:

- **Above the links.** On every article page, an affiliate disclosure line is printed **before** the article body and its product links — you cannot reach the links without passing it.
- **In the footer of every page.** The site footer repeats the Amazon Associates statement and links here.
- **On this page.** The full policy, always available.

Some of our product link buttons and badges carry `rel="sponsored"` in the markup, which tells search engines and browsers the link is commercial.

### What this means for you

- **You never pay more.** Affiliate links never change the price you pay at checkout.
- **We are not paid for placement.** Vendors cannot buy a spot, a rank, or a position in our articles. Placement is decided by our editorial standards — see our [Advertising Policy](/advertising-policy/) and [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/).
- **Not every link is an affiliate link.** We only use affiliate links where the product passed our own checks.

### How we choose what to recommend

Products appear in our articles only after they pass the research and verification process described in our [Review Methodology](/review-methodology/). Products we cannot verify are never promoted with an affiliate link. That is also why you will see us recommend things with no affiliate link attached — or no link at all.

### Future advertising

We do not currently display third-party advertising on TailWell. If that changes, this page and the [Advertising Policy](/advertising-policy/) will be updated before any ad code is added, and any advertisement will be labeled.

### Medical disclaimer

Nothing on TailWell is veterinary advice. Always consult a licensed veterinarian before starting any supplement, food, or treatment — particularly for senior, pregnant, or special-needs pets. The fuller version lives on our [Disclaimer](/disclaimer/) page.

### Questions

Questions about this policy? Contact us via the [Contact](/contact/) page.
